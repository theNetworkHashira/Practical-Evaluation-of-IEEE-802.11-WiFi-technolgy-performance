# Methodology

## Capture Context

Measurements were collected at the Faculty of Informatics building (IK) during the summer, when campus traffic was minimal because very few students were present.

## Processing Stages

1. Read two similarly structured CSV files.
2. Validate required columns.
3. Convert time and frame length to numeric values.
4. Classify frame descriptions.
5. Compute comparative metrics.
6. Export tables and figures.
7. Inspect differences while considering capture duration and traffic context.
